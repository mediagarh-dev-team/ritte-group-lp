Our Programmes

Nursing Programs at RITEE

Explore the three levels of nursing programs at RITEE: B.Sc., Post-Basic B.Sc., and M.Sc.

01 Duration: 4 Years

B.Sc. Nursing

Eligibility

Class 12 PCB + English with 45% marks (5% relaxation for SC/ST/OBC).

What you'll learn

  • Human anatomy and physiology
  • Biochemistry
  • Adult health nursing
  • Nursing information & technology
  • Professionalism, Professional Values, and Ethics
02 Duration: 2 Years

Post-Basic B.Sc. Nursing

Eligibility

Registered Nurse with CGNRC + GNM with 50% marks (5% relaxation for SC/ST/OBC).

What you'll learn

  • Advanced medical-surgical nursing
  • Maternal & child health
  • Mental & psychiatric health
  • Public health
  • Nursing administration
03 Duration: 2 Years

M.Sc. Nursing

Eligibility

Registered Nurse with CGNRC + B.Sc. Nursing/B.Sc. Hons. Nursing/PBB.Sc. Nursing with 55% marks (5% relaxation for SC/ST/OBC) + minimum 1 year of work experience.

What you'll learn

  • Advanced pathophysiology
  • Advanced pharmacology
  • Biostatistics
  • Health assessment
  • Leadership
